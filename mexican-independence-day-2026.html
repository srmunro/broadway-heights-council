Mexican Independence Day Celebration

📅 Saturday, September 12, 2026 · 🕐 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM

¡Viva México! Join the Broadway Heights Community Council for a vibrant celebration of Mexican Independence Day on the Martin Luther King Jr. Promenade.

Come out to enjoy great Mexican food, live music, traditional dancers, games, and more with your neighbors. This free, family-friendly event is open to everyone — "All means All!"

Bring your family and friends, bring your chairs, and celebrate Mexican culture, heritage, and community with us on the Promenade.

🌮

What to Bring

  • Your own chairs or blanket for seating
  • Sun protection — hat, sunscreen, shade canopy if you have one
  • Family and friends — everyone is welcome
  • An appetite for great food!

Parking & Transit

Street parking is available along M.L.K. Jr. Way and surrounding streets. The Promenade is also accessible via the San Diego Trolley (Orange Line — 47th Street Station, short walk east).

Accessibility

The Martin Luther King Jr. Promenade is a fully paved, wheelchair-accessible pathway. If you need any special accommodations, please note them when you register or contact us at info@broadwayheightscouncil.org.
