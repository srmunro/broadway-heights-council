What we do

We create opportunities for learning and leadership right here in Broadway Heights. Our work is grounded in five core values that guide everything we do.

Safety & Trust

Working with local partners to maintain our reputation as one of San Diego's safest neighborhoods through community collaboration.

Unity & Inclusion

All means All — we bring together residents, businesses, nonprofits, schools, and organizations to build a stronger community.

Leadership & Advocacy

Being a strong voice for residents with government leaders, schools, and nonprofits to ensure our community's needs are heard.

Beautification & Growth

Creating clean, vibrant, and thriving streets that make Broadway Heights a place we're proud to call home.

Civic Pride

Building community pride and making Broadway Heights a model neighborhood that sets the standard for what communities can achieve.

How We Work

Get to Know Us

Our "All means All" approach ensures every neighbor has a voice in shaping our community's future. Through partnerships with government leaders, community organizations, businesses, schools, and faith-based groups, we address the issues that matter most to residents.

We work together to make Broadway Heights a place where everyone can thrive.
